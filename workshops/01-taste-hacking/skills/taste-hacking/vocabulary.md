# Taste vocabulary

One word per idea, with how to ask an agent for it. If you know the name, you can ask for it.

## Motion

- **Easing**: the speed curve. `ease-out` starts fast and slows down, which suits things entering. `ease-in` starts slow and suits things leaving. Ask for "ease-out entrance, 250ms."
- **Spring**: motion driven by physics (mass, stiffness, damping) instead of a fixed duration. It feels natural. Ask for "a spring with no bounce."
- **Overshoot**: going slightly past the target and settling back. It adds energy, and too much looks childish.
- **Stagger**: list items enter one after another instead of all at once. Ask for "40ms stagger."
- **Duration**: most UI motion lives between 150 and 400ms. Past 500ms it feels slow.
- **Layout animation**: an element changes size or position and glides there instead of jumping.
- **Scroll-driven**: the animation advances with scroll instead of time.

## Look

- **Hierarchy**: what you see first, second and last. Size, weight and contrast create it.
- **Type scale**: sizes on a fixed ratio (12, 14, 16, 20, 24, 32) instead of loose values.
- **Tracking**: space between letters. Large headings usually look better with negative tracking.
- **Leading**: line height. "14/20" means 14px type on a 20px line.
- **Grid**: the invisible column structure that aligns everything.
- **Vertical rhythm**: consistent spacing between blocks, in multiples of 4 or 8.
- **Density**: how much information fits on a screen. Linear is dense and Apple is airy.
- **Contrast**: the difference between text and background, with accessibility minimums (WCAG).

## Surface

- **Blur and backdrop blur**: blurring what sits behind an element, the base of glassmorphism.
- **Grain**: subtle noise that keeps a gradient from looking like plastic.
- **Layered shadows**: two or three soft shadows stacked look real, while one hard shadow looks cheap.
- **Radius**: corner rounding. Consistency matters more than the value.

## Video

- **Jump cut**: a cut inside the same shot to remove pauses.
- **J-cut**: the next shot's audio starts before its picture.
- **Punch-in**: a quick digital zoom for emphasis.
- **B-roll**: supporting footage over the voice.
- **Kinetic type**: animated text that follows the rhythm of speech.

## Slop (signs a machine made it)

- A purple-to-blue gradient background.
- A generic pulse animation that communicates nothing.
- An em dash in every sentence of the copy.
- Short clipped sentences stacked for rhythm ("Fast. Simple. Powerful.").
- The wrong date or year in the footer.
- Emojis used as bullets.
- Six identical cards, each with an icon, a title and two lines.
